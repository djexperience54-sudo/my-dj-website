# PHASE 20 — POST-LAUNCH LEARNING

After deployment:

* Maintain the application.
* Add improvements.
* Study its architecture.
* Identify security weaknesses.
* Learn from real-world behavior.

The ultimate goal is for me to understand the architecture well enough to build future applications with increasingly less AI assistance.
